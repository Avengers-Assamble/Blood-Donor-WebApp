Good to know

Common questions

Is Lifeline an emergency service?

No. Lifeline supports coordination, not emergency care. For urgent medical help, contact your treating hospital or local emergency services.

Who can become a donor?

You create a profile, and the donation center confirms your eligibility before any donation.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining and using Lifeline is free.
